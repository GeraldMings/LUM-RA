GENTLE SKINCARE

Start with the simple things.

Products are only part of healthy skincare habits. Luméra can also provide simple, gentle care guidance.

Gentle Care

  • Use a gentle cleanser.
  • Keep your skin moisturized.
  • Use a cool compress when soothing is needed.
  • Consider gentle aloe-based products if tolerated.
  • Use daily sun protection.

Protect Your Skin

  • Do not place ice directly on your skin.
  • Avoid harsh physical scrubs.
  • Avoid squeezing breakouts.
  • Don't introduce many new products at once.
  • Stop products that clearly irritate your skin.
