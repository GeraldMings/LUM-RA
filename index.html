GENTLE SKINCARE

Start with the simple things.

Luméra can combine product recommendations with simple skincare habits that support your routine.

Natural & Gentle Care

  • Use a gentle cleanser instead of harsh scrubbing.
  • Keep your skin moisturized.
  • Use a cool compress for temporary soothing.
  • Consider aloe-based products if your skin tolerates them.
  • Wear daily sun protection.

Things to Avoid

  • Do not put ice directly on your skin.
  • Avoid harsh physical scrubs.
  • Avoid squeezing active breakouts.
  • Do not introduce too many new products at once.
  • Stop products that clearly irritate your skin.
