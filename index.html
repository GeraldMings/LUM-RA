🌿 Natural & Gentle Care

Simple habits that may help support comfortable, healthy-looking skin.

  • Cool compress for temporary soothing
  • Gentle cleansing without harsh scrubbing
  • Simple fragrance-free moisturization
  • Aloe-based products if your skin tolerates them
  • Daily sun protection

⚠️ What to Avoid

More is not always better when caring for your skin.

  • Avoid putting ice directly on your skin
  • Avoid harsh physical scrubs
  • Avoid squeezing pimples
  • Avoid trying many new products at once
  • Stop a product if it causes significant irritation
